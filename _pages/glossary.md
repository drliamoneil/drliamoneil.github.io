Below is a list of definitions and recommended reading for key terms used in my research.

### Actual Competence
An organism's capacity to interact effectively with its environment.

  White, R. W. (1959). Motivation reconsidered: The concept of competence. <i>Psychological Review</i>, <i>66</i>(5), 297–333. </p>
  </font>
</details>

### Exercise
Those bodily movements that are planned, structured, and repetitive, with a purpose of improving or maintaining physical fitness.

  </font>
</details>
